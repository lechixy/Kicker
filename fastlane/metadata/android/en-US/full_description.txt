<p>Kicker is an independent Android client for Kick. Browse channels and categories, search for creators, and watch live streams, recordings and clips.</p><ul><li>Watch streams and read chat as a guest. Connect a Kick account to follow channels and send messages.</li><li>Choose your video quality, use picture-in-picture or the mini player, and set a sleep timer.</li><li>Save local favorites, keep a watch history, and resume recordings.</li><li>Use the app in English or Turkish on phones and tablets.</li></ul><p>Kicker uses Kick's online services for content, account features and chat. During live playback, it sends viewing events to Kick.</p>
